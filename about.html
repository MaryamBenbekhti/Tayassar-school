Our Story & Mission

Bridging the gap between the heart and the Holy Quran.

The Meaning of "Tayassar"

In Arabic, Tayassar means "to become easy." We believe that the language of the Quran and the words of Allah should be accessible to every Muslim, regardless of their native tongue or background.


Tayassar School was founded to solve a common challenge: many non-Arabic speaking Muslims feel a distance from the Quran because of the language barrier. Our mission is to remove that barrier through simple, step-by-step, and interactive learning.

Why We Started

"It began with a simple observation: students were struggling with complex grammar before they could even enjoy the beauty of recitation. We decided to flip the script—focusing on love for the Book first, and making the rules follow naturally."

Our Mission

To provide world-class Quranic and Arabic education that is flexible, affordable, and spiritually uplifting for students around the globe.

Our Vision

To become the leading global platform where every Muslim can confidently read, understand, and live the message of the Quran.
